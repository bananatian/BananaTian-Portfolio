BANANATIAN

Game Design Student & Game Developer

I like making the things I want to make — especially the strange ones.

I love games, and that's why I love making them. I'm drawn to weird ideas, meta elements, unusual interactions, and stories that linger after the screen goes dark.

HOW I MAKE GAMES

I enjoy programming because it feels a little like painting with 0s and 1s.

I like starting with something that only exists in my head, then slowly turning it into something real — something a player can see, interact with, and experience.

WHAT I WANT TO MAKE

I want to make games that may not be for everyone, but become special to someone.

Games that make people create their own interpretations, discuss what really happened, or remember a certain scene years later.

I want my games to leave a little something behind.

Maybe a question.
Maybe a memory.
Maybe a little thorn that never quite goes away.
